# What the one-step look-ahead evidence establishes

The one-step heuristic chooses a legal edge that leaves the largest number of legal additions immediately afterward. It achieved the exact optimum on all 9,000 graphs in the original finite evaluation panel. The new exhaustive search follows **every** tied minimum-loss choice reachable from the empty graph, deduplicating labeled states by edge set. It found no failure through n=8: respectively 2, 7, 25, 186, 1,121, 12,027 and 64,464 reachable states for n=2…8. This supports a finite claim about the algorithm from its specified starting state; it is not an arbitrary-size proof. An exploratory n=9 search stopped at its declared 250,000-state cap and is incomplete.

The heuristic has a simple **partial-state counterexample** at n=6. Start with the star edges (0,1), (0,2), (0,3), leaving vertices 4 and 5 isolated. This state can be completed to K(3,3) with parts {0,4,5} and {1,2,3}. The action (4,5) has the smallest possible one-step loss, **one** legal action: no other currently legal edge becomes blocked immediately. The heuristic therefore selects it. But then the star's color-class sizes (1,3) and the new edge's sizes (1,1) can only combine into sides of sizes (2,4), not (3,3). An optimal completion is impossible; completing K(2,4) yields eight edges versus the optimum nine.

This witness is triangle-free, the look-ahead score was checked by counting the before/after legal sets, and the balanced-partition obstruction was checked by independently enumerating all 20 three-vertex sides. A separate exact continuation check enumerated all 512 subsets of the nine initially legal edges: action (4,5) has continuation maximum eight, while each of the six best feasible alternatives has maximum nine. Maximizing witnesses were checked through NetworkX. The star state was **not** found among the states reachable by tied look-ahead decisions from the empty n=6 graph. Thus it disproves a statewise greedy guarantee while leaving the empty-start arbitrary-size guarantee unresolved. The exact finite search and the exploratory witness are separate records, not extra independent evaluation episodes.
