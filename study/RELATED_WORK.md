# Literature positioning (checked 16 September 2026)

The relevant contribution must be a controlled explanation of learned construction behavior. Neither neural graph construction, cross-entropy training, message-passing limitations, nor finite size transfer is new by itself. This is a focused primary-source review, not an exhaustive novelty certification.

| Primary source | Relevance and implication for this study |
|---|---|
| [Wagner, *Constructions in combinatorics via neural networks* (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.14516) | Uses deep cross-entropy search to obtain constructions and counterexamples. CEM-based extremal graph search is established prior work. Our known-optimum task studies behavior rather than discovering a new extremal bound. |
| [Angileri et al., *A Systematization of the Wagner Framework* (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12667) | Studies graph-building environments, reward choices and architecture selection. The environment/action design is part of the method and must not be mistaken for knowledge learned by the network. |
| [Damnjanović et al., *RLGT* (2026), version 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.17276v2) | Provides a modular extremal graph RL framework and reviews earlier construction work. A reusable implementation alone has substantial existing competition; this project needs a specific empirical finding and auditable evidence. |
| [Darvariu, Hailes and Musolesi, *Goal-directed graph construction using reinforcement learning* (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.11279) | GNN-based graph construction and some larger-graph generalization already appear in prior work on robustness objectives. Broad claims of the first size-generalizing graph constructor would be indefensible. |
| [Xu et al., *How Powerful are Graph Neural Networks?* (2019)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.00826) | Establishes expressivity limits of neighborhood aggregation. The C8 argument is a concrete consequence of symmetry and this encoder's node-to-edge readout, not a new general GNN impossibility theorem. |
| [Yehudai et al., *From Local Structures to Size Generalization in Graph Neural Networks* (ICML 2021)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/yehudai21a.html) | Connects local structural shifts to size-generalization failures. Supporting variable input sizes does not ensure transferable behavior. Our parity and larger-size tests measure behavior; they do not establish an arbitrary-size algorithm. |
| [Veličković et al., *The CLRS Algorithmic Reasoning Benchmark* (ICML 2022)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/velickovic22a.html) | Provides broad algorithm-execution benchmarks. This project's narrow, known-optimum setting offers exact feasibility diagnostics, but is much narrower than an algorithmic reasoning benchmark suite. |
| [*Towards Better Out-of-Distribution Generalization of Neural Algorithmic Reasoning Tasks* (2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.00692) | Examines distribution shifts, validation metrics and a 2-WL-based processor. Pairwise reasoning and stronger relational representations are established directions; adding them here would require information-matched controls and measurable benefit. |
| [Slyvka et al., *Learning Minimally Rigid Graphs with High Realization Counts* (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12427) | Reports deep cross-entropy training with a GIN encoder and equivariant extension actions for an extremal rigidity objective. Merely combining equivariance and CEM is not a sufficient novelty claim. |

A promising narrower question is whether exact irreversible-feasibility annotations explain the gap between high average density and low exact construction success, and whether consequential action aliasing occurs during learned rollouts. The constructed C8 state makes that question precise. Actual-trajectory frequencies, matched controls, and a prospectively defined intervention are needed to support a mechanistic contribution.

Do not describe this work as a theorem discovery, a competitive solver for an unknown extremal problem, or a general neural implementation of Turán's construction. The optimum here has a direct quadratic-time construction. Publication readiness depends on what the corrected study establishes beyond this useful calibration problem.
